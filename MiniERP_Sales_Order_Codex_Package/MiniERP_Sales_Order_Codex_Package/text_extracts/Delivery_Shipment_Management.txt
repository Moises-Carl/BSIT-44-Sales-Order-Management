              Delivery and Shipment Management System

1. Purpose and System Scope
       The Delivery and Shipment Management System manages the full life of a shipment, from
booking to proof of delivery, for a delivery company and its customers.

The system is responsible for the following functions:

       The system shall support shipment booking by allowing users to create a shipment with
the sender, receiver, package details, and pickup and delivery addresses. It shall provide dispatch
and assignment features that let dispatchers assign shipments to a delivery route and to a driver
or courier. For tracking, the system shall record every status change and display the current status
and full history to both staff and customers. It shall capture proof of delivery, including the
receiver’s name, a signature or photo, and the delivery timestamp. The system shall also handle
exceptions by recording failed deliveries, damaged items, returns, and reschedules. To support
billing, it shall compute shipment charges based on weight, distance, and service type. For
reporting, it shall produce daily dispatch, on-time delivery, and exception reports. Finally, it shall
provide user and access management that controls what each role (admin, dispatcher, driver,
and customer) can view and change.

Out of scope for this version:

   •   Route optimization algorithms, which are handled by an external routing service.
   •   Payment gateway processing, which is handled by an external payment provider.
   •   Warehouse inventory management.

3. Business Rules

Shipment status
A shipment moves through these statuses in order. Only the transitions shown are allowed.

   1. BOOKED: shipment created, not yet picked up.
   2. PICKED_UP: driver scanned the parcel at the sender address.
   3. IN_TRANSIT: parcel is at a hub or on a route.
   4. OUT_FOR_DELIVERY: driver has the parcel on the delivery route.
   5. DELIVERED: proof of delivery recorded. Final status.
   6. FAILED_ATTEMPT: delivery was tried and failed; returns to OUT_FOR_DELIVERY or
        moves to RETURNED.
   7. RETURNED: parcel is returned to the sender. Final status.
   8. CANCELLED: booking cancelled before PICKED_UP. Final status.

Validation rules
   •    Every shipment must have a sender address, a receiver address, and at least one item.
   •    weight_kg must be greater than 0 and no more than 70 kg for standard service.
   •    declared_value must be 0 or greater; a shipment with a declared value above 50,000 PHP
        requires dispatcher approval.
   •    tracking_no is unique across all shipments and is generated by the system, never typed
        by staff.
   •    A status change must include a timestamp and the user or driver who made it.

Dispatch and delivery rules
   •    A shipment can be assigned to a route only when its status is BOOKED or PICKED_UP.
   •    A shipment can be on one route at a time. Reassigning it removes it from the previous
        route.
   •    A driver can carry at most 60 shipments per route.
   •    A PROOF_OF_DELIVERY record is required before a shipment can move to
        DELIVERED.
   •   A shipment in FAILED_ATTEMPT can be attempted up to 3 times. After the third failed
       attempt, it moves to RETURNED unless the customer requests a reschedule.

Billing rules
   •   Charges are computed from the service type, the chargeable weight (the higher of actual
       or volumetric weight), and the distance zone.
   •   Cancelled shipments before PICKED_UP are not billed.
