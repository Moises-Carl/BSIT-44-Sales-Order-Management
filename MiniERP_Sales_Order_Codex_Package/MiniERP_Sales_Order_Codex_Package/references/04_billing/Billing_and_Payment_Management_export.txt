1. Purpose and System Scope
1.1 Purpose
 The purpose of the Billing and Payment Management System is to calculate the amount due for customer orders, create billing records or invoices, record payment attempts and their results, and track payment status. The system will serve as the financial transaction component of the e-commerce platform, ensuring that each payment is associated with the correct order and that payment information is communicated to the Sales/Order Management System and other relevant systems.
1.2 System Scope
The Billing and Payment Management System will be responsible for the following functions:
- Bill/Invoice Creation: Create billing records based on the order ID, order total, and customer or billing details received from Sales/Order Management.
- Payment Recording: Record payment attempts, payment methods, transaction references, payment amounts, and transaction results.
- Payment Status Tracking: Monitor whether payments are pending, successful, failed, cancelled, or refunded.
- Payment Validation: Verify that payment requests reference valid billing records and that payment amounts are consistent with the amount due.
- Payment Confirmation: Provide payment results and transaction references to Sales/Order Management so that the order's payment information can be updated.
- Billing Records Management: Maintain billing and payment history for authorized users and administrators.


- Refund Recording: Record refund transactions and their statuses when refunds are supported by the project.

3. Business Rules
The following rules define the constraints and validations that the Billing and Payment Management System must follow.
Rule ID        Business rule
BR-01        Every billing record must be associated with a valid order ID provided by Sales/Order Management.
BR-02        The billing amount must correspond to the confirmed order total received from Sales/Order Management.
BR-03        The system must generate a unique billing ID for each billing record.
BR-04        A payment record must be linked to an existing billing record or order.
BR-05        The payment amount must match the outstanding amount due unless partial payments or other payment arrangements are explicitly supported.
BR-06        The system must accept only supported payment methods configured for the e-commerce project.
BR-07        A payment must not be marked as successful until its result has been verified through the agreed payment confirmation process.
BR-08        Duplicate payment requests must not create duplicate payment records or cause the same payment to be processed more than once.
BR-09        Order status and payment status must be maintained separately. Creating an order does not automatically mean that it has been paid.
BR-10        Failed or cancelled payment attempts must not be recorded as successful payments.
BR-11        A refund must reference an existing payment and must not exceed the remaining refundable amount.


BR-12        Payment status changes must be recorded and communicated to Sales/Order Management when relevant.
BR-13        Only authorized users or services may access restricted billing information or perform administrative payment actions.
BR-14        Each payment transaction must have a unique reference or another reliable method of identifying duplicate requests.
3.1 Payment Status Handling
The system will use the following statuses to identify the outcome of each payment transaction.
Status        Description
Pending        The payment request has been recorded but has not yet been confirmed.
Processing        The payment is being processed or awaits confirmation.
Successful        The payment has been verified and recorded as successful.
Failed        The payment attempt was unsuccessful or rejected.
Cancelled        The payment attempt was cancelled before successful completion.
Refunded        The applicable refund has been completed and confirmed.
